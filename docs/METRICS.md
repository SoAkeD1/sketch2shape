18 consecutive runs in one process with no restart. Default config. Regenerate with `scripts/summarize_metrics.py`.

Only 2 distinct user sketches are in this set, so these numbers show behavior on those shapes and are not a general accuracy claim.

## What each metric means
- **Ink coverage:** share of the sketch's strokes that fall on the generated object, with a 15 px tolerance. Used for retries.
- **Silhouette IoU:** filled sketch against the mesh seen from the front, both cropped and centered. Low on open or broken sketches even when the result looks right.
- **CLIP scores:** cosine similarity. Comparable between runs of this system; not percentages.

## Thresholds
`min_ink_coverage` 0.7 was chosen by viewing an earlier 18-run batch: the three flowers missing their leaves scored 0.35 to 0.47, and every other run scored 0.74 or higher. Those same sketches are in this test set, so the threshold has not been checked on separate data.
